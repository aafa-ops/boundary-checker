Split list

Every row here is a postcode, suburb or LGA that isn't fully inside one electorate — it lists each district it touches, that district's member and party, and what share of its area falls there. Why it matters: a postcode or suburb name alone can be misleading about who represents an area — e.g. before contacting an MP's office on behalf of a resident, or deciding which electorate offices to approach about a suburb, check this list first rather than assuming the "obvious" electorate is the only one involved. Click any row to jump straight to it on the map.
